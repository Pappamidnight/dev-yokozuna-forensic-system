NON-DISCLOUSURE AGREEMENT
This Non-Disclousure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Accenture and the second party
identified below ("Second Party") as Accenture employee. The term "Accenture" includes Accenture LLP
and its subsidiaries, that means any entity with or without legal personality controlled by or under the joint
control of a Consultant or Accenture LLP, a limited liability limited liability company established in Ireland
(registration number 471706) and their successors. The term "control" (or variants thereof) means the
direct or indirect ability to manage the affairs of another entity through ownership, contract or otherwise.
The Second Party agrees to the following terms:
1. Background. Will be provided to the Second Party access to Accenture's computer systems and
Accenture's information that it considers confidential, including due to the fact that it belongs to its
customers, and which (a) refers to research, development, business activities, products, software,
hardware, services, and past, present or future technical expertise of Accenture and / or its affiliate, and
(b) that have been identified as confidential or considered confidential by a reasonable person under the
circumstances ("Confidential Information").
2. Use. The Second Party may only use the Confidential Information for the designated purpose with
respect to its employment relationships with Accenture and not for any other purpose. The Second Party
agrees to comply with all applicable Accenture policies made available to you or any express statements
you have made with Accenture. Except as reasonably necessary for the purposes of its use, the Second
Party may not copy or reproduce Accenture Confidential Information without the prior written consent of
Accenture.
3. Protection. The Second Party agrees not to disclose the Confidential Information to third parties.
4. Property. Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement shall remain the property of
Accenture. Accenture does not grant any express or implied license to or under any patents, trade secrets,
copyrights, trademarks or other rights in its Confidential Information under this Agreement.
5. Return. Subject to the limitations to which the Second Party may be subject when making copies of
Confidential Information, the Second Party undertakes to return or destroy all Confidential Information
(including copies) made available to it under this Agreement at the request of the Accenture or upon
termination of this Agreement.
6. Data Privacy. In any circumstance where the Second Party accesses, processes or uses any data
relating to or identifies any natural person ("personal data") with respect to employment relationships with
Accenture, and through this employment relationship, with a client of the Accenture, the Second Party will:
(a) access, process and use such personal data only when necessary in order to perform its work under
this Agreement or to comply with applicable laws or judicial decisions;
(b) follow any instructions provided by Accenture regarding compliance with laws, regulations, court rulings
or self-regulation programs applicable to the collection, use and disclosure of personal data;
(c) follow reasonable policies and procedures to protect the privacy, integrity and confidentiality of such
personal data;
(d) notify Accenture immediately in case of any breach of the security of the same personal data and
cooperate with Accenture and / or Accenture Customer in any investigation or correction efforts;
(e) notify Accenture immediately in the event that the Second Party is required to disclose such personal
data by law, court order, summons, summons or other legal process to any person other than Accenture,
Accenture customer or other subcontractor with express authorization to receive such personal data by
Accenture or an Accenture customer; and
(f) return or destroy all personal data immediately upon termination of this Agreement or at any time during
the term of this Agreement upon written instructions from Accenture.

7. Exceptions. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits or limits the use of the Second Party from any
information (i) previously known to the Second Party (as documented in writing), prior to receipt by
Accenture, (ii) independently developed by the Second Party without the use of Confidential Information,
(iii) acquired from third parties that are not, to the knowledge of the Second Party, under an obligation to
Accenture not to disclose such information or (iv) to be made available to the public through any breach of
this Agreement by the Second Party.
8. Disclosure Requirement. If the Second Party receives a summons or any other suitably issued judicial
or administrative proceeding that requires disclosure of the Confidential Information, the Second Party
shall promptly notify Accenture and present to it the defense of such requirement. Unless the requirement
is limited, annulled or prolonged in a timely manner, the Second Party shall comply with such requirement
to the extent required by law. If so requested by Accenture (or its representative), and as may be permitted
by law, the Second Party will cooperate sensitively to oppose such a requirement.
9. Publicity. The Second Party may not use the name, trademark, trademark, logo, acronym or other
designation of Accenture externally without the prior written consent of Accenture.
10. General Provisions.
(a) This Agreement shall not invalidate the rights arising out of the employment relationship between the
Parties as well as other documents signed between the parties and intended to ensure an adequate level
of confidentiality, taking into account the nature of the work to be performed.
(b) The Second Party may not grant its rights or delegate its obligations under this Agreement without the
prior written consent of Accenture.
(c) This Agreement may be modified only by mutual written agreement between the parties. Nothing in this
Agreement is intended to give third parties any benefit or right to enforce any term of this Agreement.
